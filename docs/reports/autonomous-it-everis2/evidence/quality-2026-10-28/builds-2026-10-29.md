## B3: on-call shift, handover and assignment (Q02)

**Read.** The tool "Get on-call shift and handover (AIT)" (`b3-on-call-shift-tool.js`) is linked to the
OOB "Additional incident context AI agent". It uses `OnCallRotation.whoIsOnCall` and finds the shift
end by stepping one day at a time, then narrowing to the minute. It returns:

- who is on call now and until when;
- who takes over after the shift;
- the escalation line, with phones and emails.

**Write.** The Global workflow "Asignar a la guardia (AIT)" and its agent call the tool "Assign
incident to the on-call person (AIT)" (`b3-assign-on-call-tool.js`) in supervised mode:

- it uses GlideRecordSecure and `canWrite`;
- the assignee must be a member of the incident's group;
- a New incident goes to In Progress;
- the work note is written by the user.

## B4: alert response rule proposal (Q06)

**Built.** In AI Agent Studio:

- the workflow "Proponer respuesta a alertas (AIT)" and the agent "Alert response rule AI agent (AIT)";
- the tool "Propose alert response rule (AIT)" (`b4-alert-response-rule-tool.js`), in supervised mode.
  From one alert it builds the pattern (metric on the same CI, or on the same CI class), counts how
  often it fired in 30 days, and writes an **inactive** alert management rule with the OOB "Create
  Incident" subflow as its action. If a rule with the same filter exists, it returns that one. It
  never activates anything.

Access: user access and approved roles evt_mgmt_operator and evt_mgmt_admin, dynamic user, Otto panel
on.

| Run | Question (admin, SOW Otto panel) | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Crea una regla de respuesta para que la próxima vez que salte una alerta como la Alert0010111 se abra una incidencia automáticamente. Déjala inactiva y dime cuántas veces habría saltado en los últimos 30 días." | Routed to the workflow; it explains the proposal and asks to confirm. After "Yes" the tool refuses: no Event Management admin right in the session |
| 2 | Same, after adding evt_mgmt_admin to the approved roles of the workflow and the agent | Same refusal |
| 3 | Same, with the tool now returning the replay on refusal | Refusal, but states the filter and the replay |

**Why it refuses.** With "Dynamic user", the session keeps only the approved roles that the user
really holds. The admin account gets Event Management rights through the admin role, not through
evt_mgmt_admin, so the masked session can't create rules. This is the intended safety behaviour, and
it means the test needs a person who holds evt_mgmt_admin.

**Persona.** A demo persona "Operador AIT (EM)" (ait.operador.em, no password, evt_mgmt_admin,
evt_mgmt_operator, itil, now_assist_panel_user, Spanish) was created for this test. The owner decided to
keep it (decision P2-1, 29 October).

## B7: alert to incident carries the AI insight

The Global business rule "Alert to incident: carry AI insight (AIT)" runs on em_alert, after insert or
update, when the alert's incident changes and is not empty (`b7-alert-to-incident-br.js`). It writes
one work note into the incident:

- the alert, and its group or sibling alerts;
- the CI with its support group and owner;
- changes in the last 48 hours on the alert's CI and its group's CI, by number;
- the latest AI summary of the alert, or of the group's most severe member.

It fills the CI and group only when they are empty.

| Run | Alert (Quick Incident) | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Group Alert0010114 | INC0026052: CI SAP AppSRV01, DEMO.PI.SAP, owner Elena Soto, CHG0030111, 3 alerts, AI summary. **Defect:** the note was written twice (the alert is updated twice in one transaction). Fixed with a once-only check |
| 2 | Member Alert0010113 (load balancer) | INC0026053: one note, but "no changes": LoadBal01 has none. Member alerts now also read the group's CI |
| 3 | Member Alert0010111 (filesystem) | INC0026054: one note, CHG0030111 on AppSRV01, the 2 sibling alerts, AI summary |

This puts the change number and the owner in the hand-off, two of the Q05 gaps, without touching the
OOB agent. The AI text stays in English: autonomous notes run as the system (L7). Score 11: complete
and grounded; the mixed languages cost a point.

**Demo impact:** the three Q05 alerts now have incidents. For the demo, send fresh events (the script
already says so) and create the incident from the group.

## B8: resolution plan that cites similar incidents (Q03)

The OOB plan agent is protected and follows fixed steps, so it can't be changed or extended. Its data
fetch calls a public script include (`InvestigatingAndResolvingIncidentsUtil.fetchAllData`) that already
returns the 10 most similar resolved incidents. The OOB plan just doesn't cite them.

**Built:**

- **Tool:** "Get similar resolved incidents (AIT)" (`b8-similar-incidents-tool.js`). It reuses that
  search and adds, for each hit, the resolving group, the resolution date and the close notes. It also
  ranks the groups.
- **Workflow and agent:** the Global workflow "Plan con incidencias similares (AIT)" and its agent. The
  agent keeps only the same problem, cites the incident numbers on every step, counts the evidence,
  and checks the group.

**Test incident:** INC0026051, "No me llega la aprobación de Authenticator desde que cambié de
teléfono", created in DEMO.PI.M365 on purpose to make a misrouted MFA case.
