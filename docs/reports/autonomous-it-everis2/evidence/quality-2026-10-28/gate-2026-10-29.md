approval of RITM0010068 by Bow Ruggeri, INC0025491 resolved and closing on its own, INC0025716 waiting
for assignment.

**Defect found:** in run 2 the answer listed about 12 tickets and said there was nothing overdue, to
approve or to do (score 4). The tool selected tickets where the user was the caller **or the opener**,
and the persona had opened INC0026051 to INC0026054 and two requests for other people during earlier

After the fix, all 10 runs named the action, the overdue request and the approver. Three runs summarised
without the numbers (10).
